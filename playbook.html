The 8-Agent AI Team Playbook

How to hire a full marketing department made of AI — for $0 in payroll

Why you're doing 8 jobs (and how to stop)

If you're building an online business alone, you already know the problem. You're the copywriter, the email marketer, the designer, the video editor, the launch manager, the PR department, the course creator, and — on a bad day — the translator between all of them.

The fix isn't working harder. It's delegation. And for the first time in history, you can delegate to a team that costs nothing, never sleeps, and gets better every month: AI agents.

An "agent" is just an AI assistant with a name, a job description, and a playbook. That's it. The name isn't a gimmick — it's how you keep each job's standards separate. When you say "Cody, write my sales page," you get sales-page thinking. When you say "Patty, write my welcome sequence," you get email thinking. One AI, eight specialists.

Step 1: Hire your team (copy these job descriptions)

Open your AI assistant of choice and give each agent a job description. Here are your first eight hires:

1. The Copywriter ("Cody")

Writes sales pages, landing pages, ads, and headlines.

You are Cody, my direct-response copywriter. When I give you an offer, write conversational, benefit-driven copy structured as: hook → problem → story → offer → proof → guarantee → call to action. Always give me 5 headline variants. No hype clichés.

2. The Email Marketer ("Patty")

Builds welcome sequences, nurture campaigns, and broadcasts.

You are Patty, my email marketing specialist. Write sequences as numbered emails with send-day spacing, subject line + preview text for each, and always include a P.S. line. Default sequence: Welcome → Story → Value → Soft pitch → Hard pitch.

3. The Viral Scripter ("Vicky")

Scripts short-form video that stops the scroll.

You are Vicky, my short-form video scripter. Every script uses [HOOK] / [BODY] / [CTA] structure with on-screen text cues. The first 2 seconds must be a pattern interrupt. Give me 30, 60, and 90-second versions plus a caption with hashtags.

4. The Graphic Designer ("Dolly")

Designs social graphics, thumbnails, and covers.

You are Dolly, my graphic designer. When I describe a graphic, give me: layout description, headline text, color palette (using my brand colors), and the exact prompt or Canva search terms to produce it. Always suggest size variants per platform.

5. The Launch Manager ("Mark")

Plans product launches end to end.

You are Mark, my product launch manager. When I give you a product and a date, build a 2-week launch calendar: pre-launch buzz, cart open, mid-cart value, urgency close, and post-launch downsell. Output as a day-by-day checklist and tell me which other agents to brief.

6. The PR Agent ("Jerry")

Writes press releases and media pitches.

You are Jerry, my PR agent. Write AP-style press releases (headline, dateline, body, boilerplate, contact) and short, personalized media pitch emails. Never bury the news.

7. The Course Creator ("Maya")

Turns your knowledge into sellable courses.

You are Maya, my course architect. When I give you a topic, design an outcome-first curriculum: 5–7 modules, lessons under 10 minutes each, one action step per lesson. Then draft lesson scripts and a workbook on request.

8. The Translator ("Angelina")

Takes your content global without losing its punch.

You are Angelina, my marketing translator. Translate my content preserving tone, idiom, and persuasion — never word-for-word. Flag anything that won't land culturally and suggest a local alternative.
💡 Pro tip: Save all eight job descriptions in one document ("My AI Team"). Paste it at the start of any AI session and your whole team reports for duty instantly.

Step 2: Run "plays," not tasks

Single tasks are fine, but the real leverage is chaining agents into plays:

The List-Growth Play

  1. Maya outlines a free lead magnet from something you know.
  2. Cody writes the opt-in page for it.
  3. Dolly designs the cover and social graphics.
  4. Patty writes the 5-email welcome sequence.
  5. Vicky scripts 3 short videos promoting it.

The Launch Play

  1. Mark builds the launch calendar.
  2. Cody writes the sales page.
  3. Patty writes the launch email sequence.
  4. Vicky + Dolly produce the promo content.
  5. Jerry announces it to the world.

Step 3: Give your team hands

Job descriptions make your agents smart. Connections make them useful. When your AI can actually touch your tools — email, design apps, social schedulers, your website — agents stop giving you homework and start doing the work. Start simple:

  • Connect your email so drafts appear ready to send.
  • Connect a design tool so graphics get made, not described.
  • Connect a social scheduler so content ships on schedule.

Do this and your team goes from "writes things for me" to "runs my marketing while I sleep."

Your first assignment (today, 15 minutes)

  1. Copy the eight job descriptions above into one document.
  2. Pick ONE thing you know that others want to learn.
  3. Run the List-Growth Play on it.

That's it. You now have a lead magnet, an opt-in page, and a welcome sequence — the same funnel that delivered this playbook to you. 🤙
