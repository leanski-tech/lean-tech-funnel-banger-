## The transition logic

- **1 → 2 → 3** — a rising bounce from 99 to 107. Horns into crunk into hip-hop bounce;
  these are energy/vibe moves, so ride the phrase and cut on the beat rather than long blends.
- **4 → 5 is the money blend.** Can't Stop the Feeling and Uptown Funk are **both 8B (C major),
  two BPM apart.** That's a true harmonic match — you can run a long, clean blend and it sounds
  intentional, not lucky. That's the flex that makes people ask who's DJing.
- **5 → 6** — 115 to 119, up into the peak singalong. Key jumps (8B → 2A), so this is a
  cut/quick-blend on the vocal, not a harmonic mix. Let Whitney's intro do the work.
