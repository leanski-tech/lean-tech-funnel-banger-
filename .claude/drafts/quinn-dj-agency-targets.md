# Quinn — DJ Agency / Booking Targets (Honolulu / Oahu)

> Research only, verified via live web 2026-07-26. **No outreach without Lino's Level 3
> approval.** Ranked for an open-format private/corporate DJ with Hip Hop culture credibility
> (Berklee HOF, Floorlords) and brand-activation experience (Red Bull, Converse, etc.).
> These are DJ/entertainment companies that roster or sub-book DJs — the realistic path to
> steady bookings without cold-selling every gig himself.

## Hit these first (top 3)

1. **Pro Sound Hawaii** — prosoundhawaii.com
   - Corporate DJ/MC on Oahu: holiday parties, galas, **brand activations**, company events.
   - **Why #1:** corporate = higher pay, and brand activations are literally Lino's résumé
     (Red Bull, Converse). Strongest lane, best rate, cleanest fit.
   - Angle: lead with the brand-activation track record, not weddings.

2. **Frobaby Productions** — frobabyproductions.com
   - Full-service wedding + event DJ/MC **team** across Oahu (Waikiki, Ko'olina, resorts, Four Seasons).
   - **Why:** a team means overflow bookings they can't all cover — rosters need reliable pros.
   - Angle: offer to take overflow/destination dates; open-format range is the selling point.

3. **Spectrum Entertainment** — spectrumentertainmenthi.com
   - Honolulu mobile entertainment: weddings, corporate, proms; sound, lighting, LED, FX.
   - **Why:** established, broad booking flow, always moving events.
   - Angle: reliable open-format DJ/MC for their busy season.
