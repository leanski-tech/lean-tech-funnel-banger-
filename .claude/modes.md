# Thinking Modes

Invokable lenses that sharpen how the agents think. Type a mode name (e.g. `TRUTHMODE`,
`80/20`, `FIRSTPRINCIPLES`) and the agent applies that lens to the task.

> **Hard rule — modes never override the rules.** A mode changes *how an agent reasons*,
> never *what it's allowed to do*. `.claude/security-and-permissions.md` and the voice gate
> still win, every time. No mode publishes, sends, or approves anything. No mode makes
> client-facing output skip `SOUNDS_LIKE_LINO.md` + Frannie. A mode is a lens, not a key.

---

## Sharpen the thinking (use freely — internal or to shape strategy)

- **FIRSTPRINCIPLES** — break the problem to fundamentals; ignore copied advice.
- **80/20** — the smallest move that drives the biggest income. Bookings/sales are the metric.
- **REDTEAM** — argue the other side; attack the plan to make it stronger.
- **INCENTIVEMAP** — what is each person/system actually rewarded to do? Incentives predict behavior.
- **SPECIFICITY** — replace vague advice with concrete numbers, actions, and words.
- **CONTRASTMODE** — explain through sharp comparisons so the difference is obvious.
- **LINDYMODE** — answer from long-proven methods, not fads.
- **UNLEARN** — surface the outdated beliefs holding a decision back.
- **ANTIADVICE** — name the popular guru advice to ignore, then what to do instead.
- **LOSSAVERSION** — frame around what's lost by waiting or choosing poorly (use for Lino's
  own decisions; **not** as a fear tactic in client copy).

## Truth & bias checks (this is already how `aaron` and `frannie` work)

- **TRUTHMODE** — no glazing, brutally honest. The house default, not a special mode.
- **SHADOWMOTIVE** — name the hidden or selfish motive under a statement. Point it inward
  (check our own drafts/plans), never to manipulate a client.

## Teaching lenses (great for AI for DJs / `maya`)

- **ELI10** — explain anything like the listener is ten. Fits Lino's "DJ language, not tech."
- **SOCRATES** — teach by asking questions instead of lecturing.
- **FUTUREYOU** — answer as Lino's future self would. Reflective, for his own decisions.
