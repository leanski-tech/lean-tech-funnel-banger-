# DOES_NOT_SOUND_LIKE_LINO

The anti-pattern library. Anything here is what to **avoid** when drafting in Lino's
voice. Every caption is checked against this file before it is shown to Lino.

Two kinds of entries live here:
1. **Standing rules** — patterns that never sound like Lino (seeded below).
2. **Lino-flagged examples** — specific drafts Lino marked wrong, saved from his
   review corrections so the same mistake is never repeated.

---

## Standing rules — never do these

**AI marketing language (hard block — a draft with any of these fails):**
- delve, tapestry, elevate, leverage, unlock, unleash, seamless, elevate your...
- "unforgettable experiences", "take it to the next level", "we're excited to"
- "in today's fast-paced world", "look no further", "the perfect blend of"

**Fake authority / years-of-experience:**
- Never mention years of experience.
- Never inflate or invent an award, booking, credential, or stat.

**Over-promotion:**
- Dramatic claims Lino didn't make.
- Marketing language Lino didn't use.
- Hard-sell where the post has no real business purpose.

**Wrong register:**
- Corporate, stiff, or over-polished phrasing.
- Club-promotion framing (Lino is private/corporate events).
- Cheesy or artificial "hype man" energy.

**Made-up substance:**
- Invented handles, prices, links, reviews, testimonials, or media.
- Private conversations or emotional moments used as content.
- Any fake image or footage of Lino presented as real.
