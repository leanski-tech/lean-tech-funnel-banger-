## How to add a sample

Only add material Lino confirms sounds like him. Acceptable sources:

- Voice-note transcripts
- Captions Lino personally wrote
- Interview transcripts
- Memoir writing
- Emails written in his natural voice
- Stories he tells about Hip Hop
- DJ lessons and teaching stories
- Event memories
- Phrases he naturally uses
- Posts Lino says sound like him, or that close friends confirm sound like him

**Do not** add generic AI-generated posts. **Do not** add anything Lino flags as cheesy,
artificial, over-promotional, or inaccurate — that goes in `DOES_NOT_SOUND_LIKE_LINO.md`.

For each sample, record every field below.
